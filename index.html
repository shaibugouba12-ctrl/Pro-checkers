HOW TO PLAY

• Red moves first. Tap a piece, then a highlighted square.
• Pieces move diagonally forward on dark squares.
• Captures are mandatory. Jump over an opponent to capture it.
• If another capture is available after a jump, continue jumping.
• Reach the opposite edge to become a king. Kings can move forward and backward.
• Win by capturing all enemy pieces or blocking every legal move.
